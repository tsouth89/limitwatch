    Why you can trust this

    • Every number carries a confidence tier and a link to its source. Hover any dotted limit to read the exact source sentence.
    • We store only as-published facts. Per-day / $-value / cross-provider math is derived in your browser, never baked into the data.
    • Raw dated snapshots are public and version-controlled. The git history is the audit trail.
